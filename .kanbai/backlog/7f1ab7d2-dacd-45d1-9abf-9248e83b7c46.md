Create a reusable, highly specific card template that the user can fill in or that can be inferred safely when asking for new cards. The template should force clear scope, definition of done, exclusions, dependencies, lane, priority, and epic so future cards are small and actionable. Acceptance criteria: 1) template asks for title, definition of done, scope, out of scope, dependencies, lane, priority, and epic; 2) template is concise enough to use in one message; 3) template emphasizes that cards must be sized for 1–6 days of work.
